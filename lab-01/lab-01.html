Banana Bread Recipe

For when your bananas are starting to rot.

Ingredients

  • bananas
  • egg
  • flour
  • melted butter
  • sugar
  • baking soda

Instructions

  1. Mash bananas.
  2. Stir in the rest of the ingredients.
  3. Pour into a greased baking dish.
  4. Bake for 20 mins at 175 degrees Celsius.
  5. Check if done by poking the center with a toothpick, if it comes out clean, then it is ready. If not, put back for a couple more minutes.
  6. Enjoy!
